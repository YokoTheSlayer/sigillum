# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participants must treat one another with professionalism and empathy.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still requiring evidence for technical claims.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and licensing.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private or sensitive information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported privately through repository security channels.
